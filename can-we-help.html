Is this you?

Too busy to answer every call. Too small to pay someone to sit and answer it.

You'll know this is you if:

  • The phone rings out during the day, and you find out later
  • Whoever's nearest picks up, and the details end up in their head instead of yours
  • You're quoting in the evening because the day went on other people's questions
  • You've thought about putting someone in the office, and can't make the numbers work
  • You're the one who ends up chasing, and you'd rather be doing anything else

None of that is a work problem. You've got the work. It's what happens between the phone ringing and someone getting back to them.

What's actually going wrong

Two things, and neither of them is "we need more enquiries".

The work you've already earned is leaking.

Someone rang while all three of you were on site. They rang the next name on the list. You never found out it happened.

And the time goes on work that was never real.

Ringing people back to find out what the job even is. Turning up to price something that was never going to happen. Thinking, on a Sunday, about a quote that went quiet three weeks ago.

Getting found by more people, before either of those is fixed, just means losing work faster.

Who it isn't for

  • All your work comes from one contract or one builder. Nothing's coming in to miss.
  • You want someone to price your jobs. We never quote, never price, never take payment. That stays yours.
