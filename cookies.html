What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser so it can remember something between pages or visits. Some are needed for a site to work; others are used to track you across sites for advertising. The law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, alongside UK GDPR) says a site needs your consent for the second kind, but not the first.

What this site doesn't use

No analytics, no advertising pixels, no social-media buttons, no chat widgets, no session recording. If we add any of these later we'll update this page and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. Because this site relies on none, doing so won't affect how it works, other than the Book a call form possibly forgetting what you'd typed if you leave the page.
