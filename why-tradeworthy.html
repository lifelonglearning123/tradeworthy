What's changed

It isn't always the best trade that wins the work.

The big platforms now sell opportunities that used to come to you for nothing. Paid ads have taken much of what's left. And customers have changed — whoever gets back first usually takes the job.

None of that is about how good you are. That's the bit we're trying to fix — so an independent firm answers like a national one, and still runs like itself.

The point of view

You probably don't have a work problem.

You have a conversion problem and a time problem.

The work was already there. It rang once while you were on the tools and it died between ringing and answering. And the hours went on enquiries that were never going to land — ringing people back to find out what the job even is, thinking about work that was never going to happen.

Getting found by more people, before either of those is fixed, just means losing work faster.

What we actually do about it

Software hands you a tool and leaves you to run it.

You never become a software admin — unless you want to.

We set it up.

One call with you, and it's answering in your voice by the end of the next working day. You configure nothing.

We change it when your business changes.

New service, new area, a price change, a week off — send us a message and we do it. You never open a settings screen.

We check it's working, and we come to you.
